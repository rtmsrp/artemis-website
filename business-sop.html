    Before You Apply

    Read and understand this Business SOP before you request a business in Artemis Roleplay. Questions about these procedures should go through a Discord ticket. By operating a business in the city, you agree to follow this SOP along with city laws.

    Staff may shut down or seize a business when activity, conduct, or fairness standards are not met.

    To apply for a business, open a ticket in the ArtemisRP Discord and submit a business proposal.

    • Your proposal should explain the business type, concept, and how it serves the city.
    • Staff review proposals and may ask follow up questions before a decision is made.
    • Do not begin operating as a licensed business until staff approve your proposal.

    Activity is required to keep a business open. Minimum expectations include:

    • Strive to open during main city hours each night.
    • The owner should be the most active employee.
    • The team must complete at least 5 hours a week or the business risks termination.
    • Owners must keep the business well staffed.
    • Owners must work with staff to provide updated activity logs when asked.
    • Owners who go missing without notice for longer than 5 days risk having the business seized by the government.
    • Staff may shut down or seize a business.
    • Businesses are not allowed to strike. Withholding any core function of your business from the community counts as a strike under this rule.
    • Cool downs on organizations or factions that create issues for your business are allowed.

    Businesses must stay separate from gang conflict and criminal organization activity.

    • If gang issues or gang activities are brought into the business, the business may be seized at the owner’s expense.
    • Uniforms must be worn during all business operations.
    • Gang clothing is not permitted as part of a business uniform.

    Businesses run on a strike system. Owners set that system, but staff must approve it so it stays fair.

    • Owners and managers are responsible for managing strikes.
    • Unapproved or abusive strike systems may be rewritten or removed by staff.
    • Owners and managers must treat employees fairly.
    • Unjust or unfair treatment can lead to staff seizing the business.
    • Everyone involved with the business must be treated with respect.
    • Businesses may not be sold or transferred without prior staff approval.
    • Unapproved ownership changes can lead to seizure or disbandment of the business.

    These business procedures are subject to change and revision at any time with notice. Keep this page bookmarked and review updates when announced.
