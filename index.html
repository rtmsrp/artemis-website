    Welcome

    Before You Play

    Failure to abide by city laws may result in a temporary or permanent ban. As a community member, it is your responsibility to keep up to date on the latest city laws. By playing in Artemis Roleplay, you agree to these laws and confirm you understand them.

    Staff reserves the right to issue warnings or bans for conduct not strictly listed here when it harms the city, immersion, or other citizens. Looking for gray areas or arguing "it is technically not in the laws" can still lead to punishment.

    Artemis uses the following ban schedule. Staff may defer from this schedule when severity requires it.

    Offense Punishment
    1st ban1 day
    2nd ban3 days
    3rd ban1 week
    4th banPermanent

    Ban lengths vary with the severity of the law break. Serious breaks may skip steps.

    Combat logging may result in an immediate ban.

    • All members must be 18+.
    • English is the primary language of the city.
    • Respect staff at all times. They are trusted members of the community who uphold and enforce city laws.
    • Respect is also expected toward everyone in the city and in Discord at all times.
    • A quality mic is required. Voice changers are allowed as long as you remain audible.
    • Keep OOC drama outside of the city, including quarrels from another city. If you have an issue with a community member, open a ticket.
    • Homophobia, transphobia, racism, ableism, and sexism are not allowed in our community or city.
    • Do not DM another Discord member without their prior consent.
    • Do not DM admin or staff without permission.
    • Do not rush ticket submissions hoping for a quicker response. Doing so may cause further delays.
    • Discord voice chat is only for business raffles and similar showcases. No citizen should be in any voice channel while in the city, including Artemis voice channels or outside Discords.
    • Crosshairs, mechanical arms, and wide eyes are not allowed in city.
    • Do not shoot local doctors. Doing so can break the citizen using them.
    • You may not ask for a specific staff member in your ticket. If you have an issue with a staff member, say so in your opening message so that person does not handle the ticket.
    • By joining and participating in the Artemis Discord, you agree to Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
    • Racism, hate speech, harassment, and similar conduct may result in an immediate ban.
    • Spamming, toxicity, or aggressive behavior can result in admin punishment.
    • Be respectful with other members of the community.
    • Advertising without prior permission is not allowed.
    • Sharing in city information in Discord channels is not allowed unless the channel is meant for it.
    • Impersonation is not allowed and may result in removal from the server.
    • Use the ticket system for support. Do not DM or ping staff for support in public channels.
    • Respect the purpose of each channel and use them accordingly.
    • General chat is not the place to argue IC or OOC scenes. Open a ticket instead.

    To keep the city immersive, staff handle reports through the Discord ticket system. All players are strongly encouraged to run recording software that captures gameplay and microphone audio.

    If you are in a scene and notice a law break:

    • Let the scene play out. Do not break character.
    • Do not comment on the laws or tell the player they are breaking a law.
    • Capture the player ID so it appears in your video.
    • Create a player report ticket and submit related evidence.

    Reporting Requirements

    • Gameplay must be visible.
    • In game audio must be heard.
    • Your microphone must be heard.
    • Your clip should show context before the law break so staff can see what led up to it.
    • Include the name and ID of the player being reported when possible.
    • Visual evidence such as clips or screenshots is required.
    • If using a stream VOD, provide a link and timestamp, and only report scenes you were part of.
    • Reports should be made soon after the scene ends. Stale reports may be closed.
    • Failure to meet reporting requirements may result in your ticket being closed without notice.

    Player reports should not be abused. Many scenarios can be resolved IC through RP. Only report when a solid violation has occurred.

    Ticket contents are confidential between the ticket owner and staff. Sharing ticket contents publicly, including on stream, may result in punishment.

    If you have been banned, staff may provide proof at their discretion while protecting privacy. If a reporter wants to remain anonymous, staff will share what they can without exposing that person.

    Powergaming is exploiting in game or RP mechanics without allowing other players freedom to act or defend themselves, or forcing scenes with little to no choice.

    • Game breaking mechanics, modifications, and enhancements that give an unfair advantage are prohibited.
    • Exploiting city bugs, such as using emotes to enter locked buildings, will result in an immediate ban.
    • Using your job position to steal from a player owned business, armory, or stash, or reselling those items, is not permitted.
    • Parties in an active chase cannot repair their vehicle during the chase.
    • Paycheck farming is prohibited.
    • Doing illegal activities while on duty is also considered paycheck farming.
    • Going into the water to evade police is not allowed. If you are in the water, return to land as quickly as possible.
    • Software that improves aim, heal hacks, duping, macros, and similar tools are forbidden and can lead to a permanent ban.
    • Any application that manipulates visuals or mechanics for an unfair advantage is prohibited.
    • Staff may issue powergaming punishments for actions not specifically listed here.

    Metagaming is using OOC or external information for IC purposes.

    • Sharing information while in the city through third party methods such as Discord, Skype, or Teamspeak is prohibited.
    • Supporting streamers is encouraged, but you may not use information from a stream in your own RP.
    • Because staff cannot tell the difference between a quick hello and watching for gain, watching a stream while in the city is a violation.
    • Using player unique identifiers or OOC chat for personal gain is prohibited.
    • Stream sniping results in an immediate ban.

    Character blending (bleeding) is using information from one character on a different character who should not have that knowledge.

    • You cannot share information between your characters.
    • Transferring property or money, or assisting others in transfers to "cousins," is prohibited.

    Fail RP is not roleplaying realistically. All injuries must be properly roleplayed.

    • If you are bleeding out, you must RP as injured.
    • If your character uses a face cover and is asked to remove it, claiming it is sewn on or part of your face is not allowed.
    • Unrealistic actions while downed are prohibited, including organizing or calling out locations, using a phone or radio, calling for backup, or using /911a, /911e, /911, or /311 during an active RP scenario.

    Tackling

    Tackling is not Fail RP by default, but it is Fail RP when:

    • It is not consented between the parties and has malicious intent.
    • It happens in a shootout and you tackle then shoot.

    Players must engage in ample roleplay before shooting, killing, or using a vehicle as a weapon.

    • Randomly killing another citizen with a weapon or vehicle is prohibited.
    • There must be an RP reason to openly fight someone on the street. Fighting a friend or punching someone for fun or no reason is Fail RP.
    • Pulling a firearm should be a last resort.
    • Do not use your vehicle as a weapon. Attempts count as VDM even if unsuccessful.
    • Killing on sight is not allowed unless a specific city rule set allows it for that area or event.
    • Vehicle contact must stay reasonable for the scene, speed, and vehicle type, and should stay RP focused.
    • Repeated high speed ramming meant only to disable, destroy, force easy kills, or block RP may be treated as Fail RP or VDM.
    • If someone attacks you while you are in a vehicle, hitting them with the vehicle is only a last resort when no safer escape exists.
    • Accidental collisions are not automatically VDM and may be roleplayed out.

    Intentionally logging off to avoid consequences or confrontation is prohibited and can result in an immediate ban.

    • You cannot leave the city to save inventory or to respawn early.
    • This includes disconnecting to avoid PD custody or being looted.
    • When engaged in active RP, do not disconnect until the scenario has completely ended.
    • Do not join a scene if you do not have time to finish it.
    • Characters involved in criminal activity must be clear of law enforcement before disconnecting.
    • If you crash during a scene, contact other participants or staff. Failing to do so may be treated as combat logging.
    • Screenshot connection errors when possible and open a ticket right away.
    • Body shaming, slut shaming, sexual harassment, and hate speech are prohibited and handled by severity.
    • Keep OOC drama OOC. Discord is not the place to argue or harass anyone about IC or OOC issues.
    • Do not bring drama from another city into Artemis. Doing so can result in an immediate ban.
    • Trash talk may exist in character, but it should not become personal, OOC, or toxic.
    • Use good judgment. Staff review these scenes with discretion.
    • Staff disrespect is not tolerated. Being argumentative or hot headed with staff may result in punishment.
    • Do not contact server owners about punishments from staff. Use the proper ticket appeal process.

    NVL (No Value Life)

    You are expected to value your life at all times. If you are at gunpoint, comply with your assailant.

    • The gun must be out and pointed at you before fear is required.
    • The person who draws and aims first is in charge of that RP.
    • You must comply with hostage demands at gunpoint, even if it may result in death.
    • You do not have to value other people's lives above your own character goals, but your own life still matters.

    NLR (New Life Rule)

    If your character gets hurt and dies or passes out (on your back, not on the side), once they respawn they must forget everything that led up to that moment. Roleplay lost memory and do not return to the scene for revenge.

    Intrusive RP

    You are prohibited from placing yourself into another player's RP scenario that does not already include you. This includes metagaming into a situation by watching a citizen stream while in the city.

    Permadeath

    If you decide to permadeath a character, submit a ticket so staff can help organize the scenario. Not all roleplayers are comfortable with permadeath RP. Once people involved are selected and the scenario is approved, the character may be permadeathed.

    Green zones are crime free areas. You may not kill, rob, or perform criminal activity inside them. Green zones do not mean you can break character. Scenes that start outside a green zone and are taken into one (for example by fleeing) may continue.

    Green zones include:

    • Hospitals
    • PD, except the jail cells area
    • City Hall
    • Active events, including business events and approved public or private parties
    • Clothing stores and barber shops
    • Businesses, outside or inside

    "Sign language" (speaking with /me) is allowed. If you are not in the room with someone using sign language, you cannot overhear them. If somebody using /me has not told you their name, you are not allowed to know their name.

    • Stay in character at all times. Do not break character for any reason.
    • The only OOC talk allowed in city is when all parties consent and they are in a secluded area or housing that will not disrupt other citizens.
    • Do not go AFK in the city. If something happens while you are AFK, you are responsible.
    • Do not break immersion by asking known government officials OOC questions about city suggestions or city business. Use Discord tickets.
    • Do not threaten someone by saying you are going to "government," "the gods," or "staff." Finish the RP, then report.
    • Citing a city law while active in the city is prohibited and is breaking character. Report law breaks to staff.
    • There must be at least 15 seconds of interaction before crime RP can begin.
    • RP your injuries. If you take harm, roleplay it and give EMS room to immerse.
    • Do not impersonate real life persons, fictional or otherwise.
    • Harassment and discriminatory behavior are not tolerated. Respect other citizens.
    • Dislike does not give you liberty to be disrespectful.
    • Do not cop bait. This includes robberies, discharging a firearm, reckless driving, or false reports meant to lure PD.
    • Do not include yourself or your party in an active scene you are not already part of, such as crime scenes, robberies, or heists.
    • Do not bait others into breaking the laws.
    • Animal cruelty and abuse are not allowed, including physical and verbal abuse.
    • Public nudity is forbidden, including ERP in public. Nudity and ERP stay private and out of view of other players.
    • You cannot reveal secret locations such as drug spots to the whole city through public posts. You may give or sell locations to individuals through RP.
    • Roleplay does not stop because you went into your head. If you need to step away, move to a safe place such as your apartment or house.
    • Rape RP is prohibited, including jokes and innuendos referring to rape.
    • Erotic RP is only permitted if all parties agree and it is done in private areas.
    • Torture RP is only permitted if all parties agree.
    • Suicide RP is prohibited, including jokes and innuendos. Someone pointing a gun and demanding you jump is not suicide RP, but still must follow NVL and consent standards where required.
    • Immortal or god RP is prohibited.
    • Impersonating staff is prohibited.
    • Pregnancy RP is strictly against TOS in any way, shape, or form.
    • Abortion RP is prohibited, including jokes and innuendos.
    • If you are downed on your side and get treated, your memory is very fuzzy for the last 15 minutes.
    • If you get flat backed or see the light, you forget the last 15 minutes.
    • You cannot rejoin a scene in either case.
    • Provide adequate roleplay to EMS: act out injuries or use /me if unconscious, and respond to their actions such as sedation.
    • EMS cannot remind citizens of who killed them.
    • You cannot use the light to avoid prison time. Once engaged by LEOs, CPR is not needed for that purpose.
    • Players may only rob the same person once every two hours.
    • You cannot force victims to withdraw cash from an ATM, enter their property to be robbed, or sell property or vehicles to meet demands.
    • No pocket wiping. You may only steal gems or precious materials, illegal items or ingredients, and weapons. Job items, stones, and basic needs such as phone or food should not be taken unless RP clearly calls for it.
    • Do not target new players to scam or steal from.
    • Do not steal Mechanic, EMS, or PD supplies, or items locked to a job.
    • You may not rob a player owned business or force employees to give anything they have.
    • After committing a crime and getting away, wait ten minutes before changing outfits.
    • Do not leak locations or information for drugs, heists, and similar content.
    • You cannot take a citizen's food, drinks, phone, or radio.
    • Store clerks and bank tellers count as a hostage if you are alone. With two or more criminals, you need an actual hostage or PD can breach.
    • Motorcycles cannot be used for crime as a primary plan. Switching to one to escape is allowed if it was hidden away beforehand.
    • Doing /help during a scene before it ends and you are clear of hostile interaction or robbery will result in a ban.
    • When fleeing a chase, fully lose the pursuing party before storing your vehicle. This includes banks and other robberies.
    • Do not camp a location to rob people.
    • You cannot kill somebody you have robbed unless they keep talking smack during your scene.
    • Rat looting is prohibited. Do not rob people killed in a scene you were not involved in.
    • Do not go to the light to avoid RP. If killed in a shootout, you cannot use the light to avoid being robbed. You may go to the light if they carry you with no clear objective, and only after acting out injuries.
    • Employees who are fired or quit cannot rob the business of items or funds.
    • Notify staff via ticket if you receive a large amount of money, unapproved weapons, or a large unexpected gift. Failure to report may result in a warning or ban. This includes wealth transfers when someone leaves the city.

    Heist Limits

    • Maximum of three criminals for a small robbery. Small robberies are any robbable entity that is not a listed heist, including a bank truck.
    • Maximum of eight criminals for Madrazo's ranch heist. Other criminals may counter if law enforcement has not arrived yet.
    • Oil rig is max four people and can be started with only three cops.
    • Except for Oil rig and Madrazo's ranch, a maximum of two outside criminals (radio spotters, gunmen, extra driver, and similar) count toward the heist or robbery cap.
    • During a robbery, criminals may have one main escape vehicle at the scene. The primary vehicle cannot be a bike. Up to two backup vehicles may be hidden away. Anyone dropping off an extra vehicle must be cued in properly and count toward the criminal cap.
    • Motorcycles and bikes are allowed as a primary vehicle for petty crimes such as drug selling, crate robberies, oxy runs, and gun doors, and as a secondary vehicle for larger crimes.
    • Interceptor or interference vehicles are allowed only if multiple police vehicles are on a chase. If only one police vehicle is chasing, you may not interfere.
    • After PD arrives at bigger robberies and heists, prioritize leaving once you achieve your goals. Do not overstay just to shoot criminals or police.
    • Wait for PD response time based on location: stores generally 3 to 5 minutes, banks 5 minutes (up to 10 for Paleto), Bobcat 3 to 5 minutes.
    • Do not loot and dip robberies or heists that are not considered big, such as house robberies, stores, Fleeca, banks, cash exchange, and bank trucks.
    • Wait 20 minutes after any robbery before starting another.
    • During a robbery or heist, criminals must inform LEOs of outside help before it joins, but are not required to tell the truth about inside gunmen count.
    • Criminals must state the exact number of hostages during negotiations.
    • Criminals and police must actively negotiate during robberies, heists, and other robbable entities.
    • Criminals may not start a robbery or heist within 30 minutes of a city restart. Police should not respond to those calls and should report them to staff.

    Gangs

    • Gangs must wait until a new person has been in the city for 1 week before recruiting them.
    • All gangs must have an equal chance at recruiting someone. Consider a shared meeting with potential recruits.
    • Gang wars must be planned by the gangs involved, with a meeting covering time, win conditions, prizes, and related terms.
    • Gangs are not allowed to sit right outside a business. Stay outside business property lines. Failure can result in consequences such as flight.
    • Review Artemis gang information and SOPs for additional organization rules.
    • The longest you may hold a kidnappee is thirty minutes unless they agree to longer.
    • During an active store or bank robbery, if another player enters, you may take them hostage.
    • Criminals may not use friends as hostages for heists.
    • Criminals may take police or EMS hostage if they are off duty.
    • Criminals cannot exchange a hostage to free somebody in prison or police custody. During a prison break attempt, a hostage may be used for leverage.
    • Maximum of four criminals attempting a prison break.
    • When using a hostage at a heist or robbery, the initial hostage gets ten minutes, plus three minutes per additional hostage, before PD may breach. If any criminals leave the building, that timer is void and PD may act per their SOPs.
    • To take someone hostage you must have a gun pointed at them, take them on foot, and not do it from inside a car or bike.
    • You cannot ask someone to be your hostage. It is not a choice.
    • A driver being abducted may flee. Using the vehicle to injure or kill the kidnapper only as a last resort escape is self defense, not VDM.
    • Be courteous with hold time so you do not limit their RP.
    • Do not reuse the same hostage for back to back robberies or scenes.

    Current whitelisted jobs include Police, EMS, and DOJ.

    • You cannot impersonate LEO or EMS, including using their uniforms or vests.
    • Job specific aircraft are only for the job they belong to.
    • Whitelisted jobs can be kidnapped. You may enact gun fear on them regardless of how many are on duty, including telling EMS to leave or holding up officers.
    • Whitelisted jobs cannot be looted of job specific items for personal gain, including EMS medical kits.
    • You cannot rob officers of items labeled PD in the name.
    • Dirty cops are not tolerated. Giving or selling PD weapons or items is strictly prohibited and may result in a permanent ban.
    • An officer accepting a bribe for a lighter punishment or information is not automatically treated as a dirty cop under this rule alone.
    • Suspects must comply with officers at gunpoint per fear rules, with robbery and outnumbering exceptions where applicable.
    • Once handcuffed in custody, you cannot empty your pockets into vehicles or onto other people.
    • Officers cannot remove licenses from dead bodies. Licenses may only be removed from living criminals.

    Ocean dumping (killing a character secretly and disposing of the body in the ocean) is prohibited and is considered powergaming. The only exception is a staff involved permadeath scenario.

    These laws and guidelines are subject to change and revision at any time with notice. Keep this page bookmarked and review updates when announced.
